## Context
**Joseph Buchdahl** — sports betting analyst & sceptic based in Buxton, UK.

Science graduate of Trinity College, Cambridge. Former environmental scientist who pivoted to betting analysis after becoming a bet runner for Patrick Veitch in 1991. Has worked as a betting analyst for 20+ years.

Runs historical sports data sites: Football-Data.co.uk and Tennis-Data.co.uk.
Regular contributor to [Pinnacle Betting Resources](https://www.pinnacle.com/betting-resources/en/author/joseph-buchdahl) with 60+ articles.

### Books
- *Fixed Odds Sports Betting: Statistical Forecasting and Risk Management* (2003)
- *How to Find a Black Cat in a Coal Cellar*
- *Squares & Sharps, Suckers & Sharks: Science, Psychology & Philosophy of Gambling*
- *Monte Carlo or Bust: Simple Simulations for Aspiring Sports Bettors* (2021)
